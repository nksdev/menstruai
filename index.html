Menstrual Health Education

Menstrual Hygiene Guide

🧼 Essential Hygiene Practices

  • Change regularly: Change pads every 4-6 hours, tampons every 4-8 hours, and menstrual cups every 8-12 hours
  • Wash hands: Always wash hands before and after changing menstrual products
  • Clean properly: Wash your intimate area with plain water or mild soap, front to back
  • Avoid douching: The vagina is self-cleaning; douching can disrupt natural pH balance
  • Wear breathable fabrics: Choose cotton underwear to prevent moisture buildup
  • Proper disposal: Wrap used products in paper and dispose in designated bins

🩸 Menstrual Products Guide

Sanitary Pads

Pros: Easy to use, widely available, various sizes

Cons: Can cause rashes if not changed regularly

Best for: Beginners, heavy flow days

Tampons

Pros: Discreet, good for swimming/sports

Cons: Learning curve, TSS risk if left too long

Best for: Active lifestyle, moderate flow

Menstrual Cups

Pros: Eco-friendly, cost-effective, 12-hour wear

Cons: Initial learning curve, requires cleaning

Best for: Environmentally conscious, all flow types

Period Panties

Pros: Reusable, comfortable, leak-proof

Cons: Higher upfront cost, requires washing

Best for: Light flow, backup protection

⚠️ Warning Signs - When to See a Doctor

  • Periods lasting longer than 7 days
  • Extremely heavy bleeding (soaking through pad/tampon every hour)
  • Severe pain that interferes with daily activities
  • Irregular periods (cycles shorter than 21 or longer than 35 days)
  • Bleeding between periods
  • Sudden changes in your menstrual pattern
  • Fever or unusual discharge during period

Tips & Best Practices

🍎 Nutrition During Periods

Foods to Eat

  • Iron-rich: Spinach, lentils, red meat
  • Omega-3: Fish, walnuts, flaxseeds
  • Calcium: Dairy, leafy greens
  • Magnesium: Dark chocolate, nuts
  • Fruits: Bananas, berries, oranges
  • Whole grains: Brown rice, oats

Foods to Avoid

  • Excessive caffeine
  • High-sodium foods
  • Processed sugars
  • Alcohol
  • Fried and fatty foods
  • Spicy foods (if sensitive)

🧘‍♀️ Exercise & Movement

  • Light cardio: Walking, swimming, cycling (reduces cramps)
  • Yoga: Child's pose, cat-cow, reclining twist
  • Stretching: Gentle stretches for lower back and abdomen
  • Avoid: High-intensity workouts if experiencing severe cramps
  • Listen to your body: Rest when needed, move when comfortable

😴 Sleep & Rest

  • Aim for 7-9 hours of quality sleep
  • Maintain consistent sleep schedule
  • Use heating pad for comfort before bed
  • Sleep in fetal position to reduce cramps
  • Create a relaxing bedtime routine

💆‍♀️ Stress Management

  • Practice deep breathing exercises
  • Try meditation or mindfulness
  • Engage in hobbies you enjoy
  • Connect with friends and family
  • Journal your thoughts and feelings
  • Limit stressful activities during periods

🌡️ Temperature Therapy

  • Heat therapy: Apply heating pad or hot water bottle to lower abdomen
  • Warm bath: Soak in warm water with Epsom salts
  • Duration: 15-20 minutes at a time
  • Safety: Avoid excessive heat, use protective layer

Myths vs Facts

❌ MYTH

You can't exercise during periods

✅ FACT

Light to moderate exercise can actually reduce cramps and improve mood. Listen to your body and adjust intensity as needed.

❌ MYTH

You shouldn't wash your hair during periods

✅ FACT

There's no medical reason to avoid washing hair during menstruation. Maintain your regular hygiene routine.

❌ MYTH

Periods sync when women live together

✅ FACT

Scientific studies have not found evidence for menstrual synchrony. Overlapping periods are likely coincidental.

❌ MYTH

You lose a lot of blood during periods

✅ FACT

Average blood loss is 30-40ml (2-3 tablespoons) per period. It may seem like more due to other fluids mixed in.

❌ MYTH

Irregular periods always mean health problems

✅ FACT

Irregular periods can be normal, especially during puberty and perimenopause. However, persistent irregularity should be discussed with a doctor.

❌ MYTH

You can't get pregnant during your period

✅ FACT

While less likely, pregnancy is possible if you have sex during your period, especially with shorter cycles.

❌ MYTH

PMS is just "in your head"

✅ FACT

PMS is a real medical condition caused by hormonal changes. Symptoms are physical and emotional, not imaginary.

❌ MYTH

Tampons can get lost inside you

✅ FACT

The cervix prevents tampons from going beyond the vagina. They cannot get "lost" but should be removed regularly.
